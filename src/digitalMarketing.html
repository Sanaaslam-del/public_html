WHAT IS A DIGITAL MARKETING COURSE

Digital marketing is the process of promoting products or services online — through Google, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and other platforms. At VarteXSoft, we teach this in a practical way rather than through slides and theory. You'll actually set up ad accounts, run campaigns, and track results as you go, so by the end you have real, usable experience rather than just notes.

This course works well whether you're a student figuring out your career path, a freelancer wanting more clients, or a business owner who wants to run your own campaigns instead of paying an agency every month.
